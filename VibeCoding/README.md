### ***Scale your AI-driven projects without the chaos.***

**VAP** is a high-integrity development framework designed for Product Owners and Engineers using AI (Cursor, Copilot, Windsurf). It applies 20 years of Engineering Management wisdom to the era of "Vibe Coding."

## **💎 The Vision**

AI makes us fast. VAP makes us sustainable.

By enforcing context reduction and architectural constraints, VAP prevents your project from becoming "software mud" and ensures it remains a wealth-generating asset.

## **🛠 The Starter Kit**

- **VIBE_MANIFESTO.md**: The AI Constitution. Feed this to your LLM to set strict behavioral guardrails.
- **vap_audit.py**: A language-agnostic auditor that maps symbols and detects logical duplication.
- **The Context Manifest**: A protocol to verify exactly what the AI knows before it writes code.

## **🔄 The VAP Workflow**

1. **Blueprint:** AI scans the index and declares systemic impact.
2. **Build:** Clean, modular implementation.
3. **Audit:** Run vap_audit.py to ensure **0% Duplication**.
4. **Clean:** Refactor, purge dead code, and update the system map.

## **📈 Why use VAP?**

- **Context Efficiency:** Spend fewer tokens and get more accurate results.
- **Anti-Fragility:** Projects don't collapse as they grow.
- **Professional Grade:** Even as a solo PO, your codebase will look like it was built by a senior engineering team.
